# Airline Competitive Landscape Analysis: Southwest vs Delta & Spirit

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of the U.S. airline industry to evaluate **Southwest Airlines’ competitive strategy** relative to **Delta (legacy carrier)** and **Spirit (ultra-low-cost carrier)**.

Using U.S. Department of Transportation airfare data, the analysis investigates pricing behavior, market leadership, and structural advantages across distance bands and time.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Assess whether legacy carriers can sustainably match Southwest’s pricing
- Examine why ultra-low-cost carriers fail to overtake Southwest in market share
- Analyze how competitive dynamics changed before, during, and after COVID-19
- Identify structural advantages in route dominance and customer preference

---

## 📊 Dataset
- **Source:** U.S. Department of Transportation
- **Coverage:** 1,000+ top city-pair markets
- **Time Range:** 1996 Q1 – 2025 Q1
- **Key Variables:**
  - Average fare
  - Fare per mile
  - Passenger volume
  - Route distance
  - Market share leader
  - Lowest-fare carrier
 
- Dataset source: https://data.transportation.gov/Aviation/Consumer-Airfare-Report-Table-1-Top-1-000-Contiguo/4f3n-jbg2/about_data

---

## 🔍 Key Analyses
### 1. Pricing Strategy Comparison
- Delta vs Southwest fare-per-mile trends
- Evidence of temporary price matching vs long-term premium positioning

### 2. Low-Cost Carrier Dynamics
- Spirit’s pricing advantage vs limited market leadership
- Distance-based widening of fare gaps

### 3. Market Dominance & COVID Impact
- Route dominance over time
- Shifts in passenger preference during COVID
- Post-pandemic recovery patterns

---

## 📈 Methods & Tools
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Hypothesis-driven visual analysis
- Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn)
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Southwest maintains market leadership despite not always being the lowest-cost carrier
- Delta’s fare reductions were short-lived and strategically reversed
- Spirit’s low prices are offset by service and customer experience limitations
- Southwest benefits from structural advantages in route dominance and customer trust
